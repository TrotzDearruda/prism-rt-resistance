Third-party material referenced or redistributed by this release

Python packages
---------------
The release depends on NumPy (BSD-3-Clause), SciPy (BSD-3-Clause), scikit-learn (BSD-3-Clause),
PyTorch (BSD-3-Clause), PyYAML (MIT) and pandas (BSD-3-Clause), each under its own licence and its own
copyright holders. No package source is vendored here.

Public data resources
---------------------
The auxiliary comparison reads resources that remain under the terms of their own repositories. They
are not redistributed with this release; the reading code fetches them from their source services and
records a per-resource ledger.

  TCGA-COAD, TCGA-READ      National Cancer Institute Genomic Data Commons, open tier.
                            Data use terms: NIH Genomic Data Sharing policy.
  PDC000116 (CPTAC-COAD)    National Cancer Institute Proteomic Data Commons.
                            Underlying publication: Vasaikar et al., Cell 2019.
  GSE132668, GSE119409, GSE46862, GSE35452, GSE45404, GSE68204
                            NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus, public access, terms as declared by each
                            submitter. Each series remains attributed to its own submitting group.

Mentioned works
---------------
The articles listed in the reference list of the accompanying paper are cited by that paper. No text
or figure from them is reproduced in this release.

Clinical data
-------------
The retrospective and silent cohorts, and the cell-line panel of the in-vitro arm, are held by the
participating institutions. They are not included here and no attempt is made to reconstruct them.
